## Phase Goal

**As the** helmsman of the X35 (the owner, racing at night), **I want to** race to the next mark on laylines alone, with polar targets, live laylines and the course on a night-readable tablet helm view over an offline map, from live or manually entered wind, **so that** I have a guaranteed "steer this / tack here" even if routing never ships.

## Acceptance Criteria (this plan)
- [ ] Marks are added, moved, edited (side, radius, name), reordered and deleted from the tablet map; the course can be shortened at any checkpoint
- [ ] The active mark auto-advances after rounding with a banner and Annuler; manual next/previous always works
- [ ] Course state survives restarts and kills; concurrent edits are detected
- [ ] A closed-loop simulated boat proves laylines, tack, rounding and auto-advance together

<objective>
Complete course and mark management (CRSE-01..05) and the closed-loop simulator, per CONTEXT §Course & marks (tap to add, drag to move, sheet with side/radius/delete/reorder, one-tap A/B, next/prev, "Finir ici", auto-advance after 10 s with "Annuler" for 60 s, persistence via atomic JSON, REST + WebSocket push) and §Simulation (closed-loop L2 boat), plus RESEARCH Patterns 6-8 and Pitfalls 10, 11.

Purpose: the course changes at the briefing ("The Organizing Authority reserves the right to change the course") and the NoR allows an auxiliary finish at any checkpoint, so the navigator must edit it at night in seconds; an auto-advance that misfires or forgets the active mark after a power cut would point the laylines at the wrong island.

Output: the complete CourseStore (editing + rounding state machine), the course REST API, the Carte editing UI with the global advance banner, and `jabuka-sim --follow` with an end-to-end test.
</objective>
