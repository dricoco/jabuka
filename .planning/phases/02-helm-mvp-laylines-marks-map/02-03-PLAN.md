## Phase Goal

**As the** helmsman of the X35 (the owner, racing at night), **I want to** race to the next mark on laylines alone, with polar targets, live laylines and the course on a night-readable tablet helm view over an offline map, from live or manually entered wind, **so that** I have a guaranteed "steer this / tack here" even if routing never ships.

## Acceptance Criteria (this plan)
- [ ] Manual TWD/TWS (°M or °T) keeps the laylines working when the bus is silent; LIVE wins whenever fresh instrument wind exists
- [ ] True wind is TRUE, damped, cross-checked against the Hercules, and switches to COG inside the Jabuka zone
- [ ] Laylines support polar or sailed angles, optional set/drift, actual-track countdown, overstood time and fetching
