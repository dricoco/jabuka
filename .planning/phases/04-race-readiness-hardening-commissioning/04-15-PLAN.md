## CR-01: which edits keep the suppression and which clear it

An Annuler suppression (`suppress_idx`) belongs to one leg: the leg the crew said is not rounded yet. The rule is that the suppression stays exactly as long as that leg stays the active leg and the boat must still round it.

**Kept** (`_carry_suppress` puts it back, or the command never cleared it). In each case the active index, the active mark and the "not the finish" status are unchanged:
- add_mark after the active leg: « Ajouter » at the default position (`insert_at=None` = active_idx + 1), and « À la fin » when the active leg is not the finish;
- delete_leg of a later leg, including the finish leg (finish_idx falls back to None);
- reorder that leaves the active leg at its index (later legs only);
- set_finish(idx) with idx > active_idx (« Finir ici » at a later checkpoint), and set_finish(None) (« Parcours complet »);
- activate(active_idx) (« Rendre active » on the active leg);
- select() that keeps the same mark at the same index (A↔B at Plić Sestre or Jabuka);
- edit_mark, set_leg and confirm_sides (they never cleared it; unchanged).

**Cleared** (the guard does not carry it):
- « Suiv. »/« Préc. » (`_step`) and activate of another leg: the crew picked another mark;
- set_finish(active_idx) (« Finir ici » on the active leg): the active leg becomes the finish, and the finish fires on entering its radius. A kept suppression would block that while the boat is in the zone, because the radius is inside the zone;
- set_finish(idx < active_idx): the active mark moves back;
- delete_leg of the active leg, or the add_mark IN-03 case (a mark inserted before the active finish becomes active): another mark is active;
- a later-leg delete that leaves the active leg as the last leg: it becomes the finish;
- an index shift of the active leg (a leg inserted, deleted or reordered before it): `_set` already resets the whole state machine there. That is residual R6, unchanged, and the crew uses « Suiv. »;
- a select() that puts another mark active (custom → A, or Blitvenica ↔ Mulo);
- `undo` sets its own suppression, and `_advance`/`_finish` change the active leg or finish the course: the guard never applies to them.

**Why central in `_commit`:** every edit command goes through `_commit`, and the carry must happen before `_write` so that course.json persists it. `_save_quiet` is the path where `_fsm` lifts the suppression on the zone exit. It does not go through `_commit`, so the stand-in still runs once and the suppression is still lifted. The carried state then behaves exactly like "no edit". It adds no new false-advance class (R10 exists with or without the edit) and no new stuck class.

## WR-01 → accepted residual R10

At a normal mark, the CR-02 stand-in sets `_near = True`. The normal rule then accepts any exit with `dot_out > 0`, including an approach-side exit on the inside of the turn (`dot_in < 0`, `dot_out > 0`), although the boat never came within radius + MARK_TOL_NM.

The alternative is to collect real `_near` on the suppressed ticks. That leaves an Annuler tapped beyond radius + 0.1 nm, after a correct advance, stuck on the beat-out. The owner rule forbids that trade.

The post-race candidate, which must be measured on the stuck grids first: collect real `_near` on the suppressed in-zone ticks, and use the stand-in only when the boat was already past the mark along the outgoing leg (`dot_out > 0`) at the first suppressed tick.
