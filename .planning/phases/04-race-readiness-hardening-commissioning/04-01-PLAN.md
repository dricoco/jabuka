## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 (verbatim): "The system runs about 36 h unattended at night on the Pi 3B, and on 12–13 Nov the owner can commission it on the real boat in one session by following a written runbook."

This plan covers the first half: an automated, reproducible soak that proves the stack survives about 36 h of faults. Plan 04-03 runs the full 36 h-equivalent and adds the Pi `--systemd` mode. Plan 04-02 covers the runbook.

## Acceptance Criteria (this plan)
- [ ] `python -m jabuka.tools.soak --scenario ci` exits 0 on the laptop. Its report shows every fault kind injected once and recovered within budget, or SKIPPED with a reason when the host cannot do it.
- [ ] The CI soak is part of `bash scripts/check.sh` on both interpreters and adds no more than about 6 minutes to the gate, together with 04-02's rehearsal test.
- [ ] Every defect found by the soak is fixed. Each fix has a RED regression test committed before it, and each is listed in the SUMMARY "Soak findings" table.
