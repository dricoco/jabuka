## CR-01: the split, and why it is exactly this

**What the evidence depends on (code read, confirmed by the reviewer and the verifier):**
- `_beyond` comes from `dot_in`, which uses the previous mark and the active mark (`_leg_dots`).
- `_near` and `_entered` come from the distance to the active mark and the leg radius.
- `_suppressed_in_zone` uses the active mark's zone, i.e. its radius.
- The side and the next mark are read fresh on every tick, by `_abeam` (turn, hairpin side rule) and by `_past_mark` (turn, `dot_out`).

So an outgoing-only change cannot invalidate the stored evidence. It can only invalidate a candidate that was judged against the old outgoing leg, so that change restarts the 10 s debounce.

**Rules:**
- Key `_fsm_geometry(idx)` = `(idx, leg.mark_id, leg.radius_nm, pos(idx - 1), pos(idx))`. On change: `_reset_fsm()`.
- Outgoing `_fsm_outgoing(idx)` = `(leg.side, pos(idx + 1))`. On change only: `_cand_since = None`.
- `_set` still resets on an `(active_idx, mark_id)` change, unchanged. An index shift (a leg inserted or deleted before the active mark) therefore still restarts everything. It is listed in R6.
- If an edit turns a normal mark into a hairpin, keeping `_beyond` adds nothing that the unedited hairpin rule (`_beyond and dot_out > 0`, read fresh) does not already allow (review CR-01).

## WR-01: Annuler in the zone at a normal mark

Since 04-13, the CR-02 path (the boat leaves the zone it was suppressed in after Annuler) forces `_beyond = True`. The non-hairpin rule starts with `_beyond or ...`, so an exit in any direction then advances, including an approach-side retreat. That reopens G-4-R02.

Annuler is tapped exactly when the crew thinks the mark is not rounded yet. The fix (review WR-01, orchestrator scope):
- At a normal mark with a usable outgoing leg, the stay in the suppressed zone stands in for "came near" (`_near = True`). The exit then needs `dot_in > 0` or `dot_out > 0`, which both G-4-R02 retreats fail.
- At a hairpin, or without a usable outgoing leg, `_beyond = True` as before, so the hairpin CR-02 scenario is unchanged.

Not chosen: collecting real latch/near evidence on the suppressed ticks (the reviewer's "better fix"). If Annuler is tapped already beyond radius + 0.1 nm after a correct advance, that variant leaves a beat-out (`dot_in < 0`) stuck. The stand-in cannot.

Boundary note: a retreat exactly along the extension of the incoming leg at a turn of exactly 90.000 deg has `dot_out` equal to +6e-17 (float). The verifier's noiseless `wr01_committee_in_zone(90)` hits it (2 advances). Real GPS cannot hold that line for 10 s. The regression test retreats at b_in + 210 deg, inside the approach quadrant for turns of 45, 60 and 90 deg.
