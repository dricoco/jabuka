## User story (MVP framing)

**As a** boat owner racing the X35, **I want to** leave the Pi 3B running about 36 h unattended at night and commission it on the real boat on 12–13 Nov in one session by following a written runbook, **so that** the helm tablet shows trustworthy live wind and laylines for the whole race even when parts of the data chain fail.

(This restates the ROADMAP Phase 4 goal, as in 04-VERIFICATION.md. ROADMAP.md is not edited.)

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 (verbatim): "The system runs about 36 h unattended at night on the Pi 3B, and on 12–13 Nov the owner can commission it on the real boat in one session by following a written runbook."
