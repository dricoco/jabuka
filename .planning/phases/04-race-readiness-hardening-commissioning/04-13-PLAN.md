## The CR-01 rule: decision and justification

**Rule.** For a mark whose turn is at most `HAIRPIN_DEG` (or with no usable outgoing leg), a zone exit counts as the rounding when:
- `_beyond`: the boat has been past the mark along the incoming leg (`dot_in > 0`) at any tick since entering the zone (or CR-02 set it after Annuler); or
- `dot_in > 0` now (as in 04-09); or
- `dot_out > 0` now **and** `_near`: since entering the zone, the boat has come within `leg.radius_nm + MARK_TOL_NM` of the mark, with `MARK_TOL_NM = 0.1` nm (185 m).

Hairpins keep `_beyond and dot_out > 0`. No incoming leg keeps the zone exit alone.

**Why this exact rule.**
1. **The latch alone is not enough.** A committee mark entered *e* metres beyond the real buoy is never passed along the incoming leg (the verifier's 19-93 m cases), so `_beyond` never sets. The boat's only evidence is leaving along the next leg.
2. **The reviewer's bare `dot_out > 0` is too wide.** On the approach side, `dot_out > 0` and `dot_in < 0` hold together in the wedge on the inside of the turn (Plić: bearings 251-294 deg from the mark). In the planner prototype, the reviewer's rule advanced every inside-of-the-turn retreat: Plić from 0.45 nm out to 255/270/290 deg, and a committee mark from 0.2 nm. That is the verifier's open point.
3. **Only distance separates the two.** In the frame of the entered point, "round a buoy *e* short of it at radius *r*" and "cross the incoming line *e − r* in front of it, then leave on the inside of the turn" are the same track. No rule based on the entered point's half-planes can tell them apart. The only quantity that differs is how close the boat came.
4. **Why 0.1 nm.** `MARK_TOL_NM` (0.1 nm beyond the leg's rounding radius) covers a tapped or typed mark error up to about 0.1 nm. That is the verifier's 19-93 m, and the planner checked 0.08 and 0.1 nm. It stays well inside `ZONE_MARGIN_NM` (0.25), so every zone keeps a 0.15 nm band where an approach-side retreat never advances, whatever direction it leaves in.
5. **Near the mark, the exit direction still decides.** A retreat straight back or to the outside (the G-4-R02 NNW/ENE repros: `dot_out < 0`) never advances, even when near.
6. **The latch at normal marks is consistent with `_abeam`.** Past the abeam line *is* "past the mark" by the `_abeam` definition (`dot_in > 0`). A tight rounding that spends less than 10 s past it therefore counts when it leaves the zone.
7. **The hairpin is untouched.** At Jabuka the approach side is also "past along the outgoing leg", so the near/outgoing term would reopen G-4-R02 there.

**Accepted residual (pinned by a test).** A boat that comes within radius + 0.1 nm of a normal mark on the approach side, then leaves the zone on the inside of the turn, advances. At that distance this cannot be told apart from rounding a mark entered up to 0.1 nm off. The 60 s Annuler banner covers it, and so does manual "précédente".
